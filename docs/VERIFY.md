# Verification items and unknowns

The following items are intentionally left as placeholders and must be confirmed before launch or before presenting this plan as fact.

- Real legal advice on waiver enforceability, data retention and required consent language for each jurisdiction in scope.
- Actual Stripe/Paystack/PayPal rate cards and settlement timing for supported countries.
- Exact SaaS hosting, database and support costs for the chosen infra provider.
- Real customer acquisition costs and trial-to-paid conversion data from live pilots.
- Definition of the specific billing logic for overage charges and annual commitment terms.
- Exact app feature set behind the trial flow, including whether templates, QR poster generation, and admin metrics are all shipped before sales begins.
- Whether a cPanel host is acceptable for the production build or whether a managed VPS/container deployment should be used instead.
- Whether QSign will be deployed under the AGPL obligations with a public source link and notice retained.

## Unknowns that must be checked

- Local market pricing sensitivity in Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa and other target markets.
- Average number of signatures per active business per month.
- Share of customers who truly need M-Pesa support versus card-based card payments.
- Whether partnerships or resellers will reduce CAC or require a referral commission model.
