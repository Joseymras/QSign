# QSign offer

## Single-sentence offer

QSign helps fitness and activity businesses replace paper waivers with a mobile-first, low-friction digital signing flow that gets new members signed, tracked, and retained without extra admin work.

## Three core promises

1. Faster check-in for members and staff
   - Members scan a QR code, sign on their phone, and are done in under a minute.
   - Staff spend less time collecting forms and chasing missing signatures.

2. Safer, simpler compliance
   - Every waiver is stored, traceable, and exportable.
   - Owners retain control of their records and can quickly show a signed record when needed.

3. Revenue-friendly growth for the studio
   - The business can start with a free trial and grow into a paid plan without changing tooling midway.
   - A QR poster and template workflow support lead generation and activation without a heavy setup process.

## Three objections and plain answers

### 1. "My staff will not use a digital waiver system."
Answer: QSign is designed to be easier than paper. The staff flow is short: choose a template, print the QR poster, hand the member a phone-friendly sign flow, and export records if needed. There is no requirement for a full onboarding project or complicated training.

### 2. "Our members do not have smartphones."
Answer: Most gyms and studios already see members with phones at check-in. QSign is built for the mobile default: a QR code is enough, and a printed alternative can still be supported if a member needs help. The product is designed around the real behavior of active members, not the exception case.

### 3. "Is it legal and safe?"
Answer: Waivers are only as good as the legal review and the process you follow. QSign makes the collection, storage, and export process easier to manage, but local waiver law and consent requirements still need a lawyer or compliance check. The platform makes compliance easier, not automatic.

## Pricing and billing structure

### Free trial
- 14-day trial, no card required.
- Includes a starter template and QR poster generation.
- Trial ends with a clear reminder and a no-pressure upgrade path.

### Starter
- Intended for one location or a small operation.
- Hypothesis price: $29/month.
- Includes: one business profile, core waiver templates, QR poster, and basic reporting.

### Studio
- Intended for multi-location or scaling studios.
- Hypothesis price: $79/month.
- Includes: more templates, multi-user access, higher signature volume, and upgraded reporting.

### Overage
- Hypothesis charge: $0.25 per signature beyond plan cap, billed monthly.
- Designed for seasonal spikes and heavy-usage months.

### Annual billing
- Hypothesis discount: 15% off annual billing for Starter and Studio plans.
- Equivalent to 2 months free on a 12-month commitment.

### Founding customer deal
- Hypothesis price: 50% off the first 12 months for the first cohort of customers.
- Exchange: a testimonial, a public case study (with permission), and a referral introduction to one other local business.
- This deal is a placeholder until real pilot data confirms demand and conversion.

## Revenue model summary

This is a simple recurring revenue model for a fitness/activities SaaS. Customers pay monthly, use the product to collect waivers, and grow in value through more locations and more signatures. The business is not built on one-off contracts; it is built on ongoing usage and retention.
